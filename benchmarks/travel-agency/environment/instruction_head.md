## Policy

# TripDesk policy (v1)

TripDesk is an online travel platform. It sells flights, rail, hotels and rides from other companies (vendors)
and supports the customer after booking. Every sentence below carries its strength (MUST or SHOULD) and its id.
Only MUST sentences are rules a write can break; SHOULD sentences are judged, never gated.
Figures tagged [V1] or [V2] are real; anything tagged [synthetic] is invented for this world.

## 00 Liability model

- [MUST P-LIAB-1] Law decides what a vendor owes the customer, and no vendor contract or TripDesk policy can take a legal right away: an entitlement compute_entitlement gives is owed even where the vendor's fare_rules say less.
- [MUST P-LIAB-2] The vendor contract fills what law leaves open and decides vendor-side refunds, changes and charges: flight_cancel, flight_change, rail_cancel and ride_cancel_scheduled apply the vendor's fare_rules, and hotel_cancel the property policy the reservation carries.
- [MUST P-LIAB-3] TripDesk policy governs only TripDesk's own actions and money, and TripDesk never grants a vendor-side refund the vendor has not granted.
- [SHOULD P-LIAB-4] When two layers disagree, the agent should follow the layer that owns the question and record the conflict with add_note.

## 10 Law and regulation

- [MUST P-DOT-REFUND] [V2] On a flight within, to or from the US (region US, or INTL for an international flight to or from the US), if an airline cancels a flight or makes a significant change (3 hours or more domestic, 6 hours or more international) and the passenger does not accept the alternative, the passenger is owed a cash refund including taxes and ancillary fees: issue_refund pays the refundable amount quote_cancellation gives for the platform_booking_id (the booking's total, taxes and ancillary fees included, less only a fee P-DOT-AGENT-FEE lets the ticket agent keep).
- [MUST P-DOT-24H] [V2] The 24-hour cancellation rule binds airlines only and never a ticket agent such as TripDesk, so on a booking whose payment has merchant_of_record platform it never makes issue_refund pay cash for a value flight_cancel returns as a credit.
- [MUST P-DOT-AGENT] [V1] A ticket agent that is merchant of record refunds the customer: where the booking's payment has merchant_of_record platform, issue_refund pays the refund to that payment_id, to a card within 7 business days of the airline's eligibility notice, other payments within 20 calendar days.
- [MUST P-DOT-AGENT-FEE] [V1] On a US booking, the refundable amount quote_cancellation gives keeps back the ticket agent's own fee only if the fee is per passenger and was disclosed as non-refundable at purchase.
- [MUST P-DOT-CASH] [V1] Where the DOT rule gives a cash refund, a vendor's own figure for the refund time, a credit at the vendor's discretion or a shorter credit validity never replaces the DOT figure: issue_refund pays it in cash whatever refund_form the vendor's fare_rules give, to wallet credit only as IP-14 allows.
- [SHOULD P-DOT-NODELAY] [V2] No US federal rule requires cash compensation for a delay, or for a missed connection caused by a separate booking.
- [MUST P-EU261] [V1] EU261 covers flights only, never trains: flights departing from an EU airport on any carrier, and flights arriving into the EU on an EU carrier. For a cancellation or denied boarding the operating carrier owes 250 EUR for flights of 1500 km or less, 400 EUR for intra-EU flights over 1500 km and other flights of 1500 to 3500 km, and 600 EUR for all other flights, unless the cause of a cancellation was extraordinary; boarding denied against the passenger's will is always compensated. The figure is per passenger, in EUR, set by the flight's distance and never by the booking's total or any vendor booking amount: compute_entitlement gives it times the booking's pax. The 2004 text of EU261 governs every case before 2027-10-23 (formerly P-EU261-TEXT).
- [MUST P-EU261-NOTICE] [V1] No compensation is owed for a cancellation notified at least 14 days before departure, or 7 to 14 days before with re-routing departing at most 2 hours earlier and arriving less than 4 hours later, or under 7 days before with re-routing departing at most 1 hour earlier and arriving less than 2 hours later.
- [SHOULD P-EU261-REDUCE] [V1] The carrier may halve the compensation when the re-routing it offered arrives within 2, 3 or 4 hours of the original arrival for the 250, 400 and 600 EUR bands.
- [MUST P-EU261-CASELAW] [V2] TripDesk applies CJEU case law (Sturgeon, C-402/07 and C-432/07): an arrival delay of 3 hours or more is compensated as a cancellation, unless the cause was extraordinary.
- [MUST P-EU-RAIL] [V1] An EU rail passenger is owed 25 percent of the ticket price (the booking's total) for a delay of 60 to 119 minutes and 50 percent from 120 minutes, paid within one month of the claim; the carrier may set a minimum payout of at most 4 EUR per ticket, and nothing is owed if the passenger was told of the delay before buying or the delay came from extraordinary circumstances outside the railway, the passenger's own fault or a third party.
- [MUST P-DB] [V1] Deutsche Bahn pays no delay compensation under 4 EUR (the min_payout in its fare_rules), and a claim expires 1 year (365 days, the claim_window_days in its fare_rules) after the ticket's validity ends.
- [MUST P-IR] [V1] A full Indian Railways refund for a train more than 3 hours (180 minutes, the tdr_late_min in its fare_rules) late is due only if the TDR is filed before the train's actual departure.
- [SHOULD P-HOTEL-LAW] No statute here gives a refund when a hotel cancels; vendor contract and TripDesk policy govern that case.

## 60 Card disputes, EU261 care and travel insurance

Background: a chargeback is a card dispute the cardholder files with the card issuer against a charge TripDesk
took as merchant of record; the card network (Visa, Mastercard) decides it. A travel insurance policy sold with a
booking is a contract between the customer and the insurer, and TripDesk is not the insurer.

- [MUST CB-OPEN] [V1] While a chargeback get_chargebacks lists is open (the payment's dispute_status open), the card network decides the money: a cardholder is never credited twice through both a dispute and a credit, so the agent never refunds that booking with issue_refund or issue_refund_legacy (issue_refund already refuses it) and answers the dispute only with respond_chargeback, once.
- [MUST CB-CLOSED] [V1] An accepted, won or lost chargeback closes the money question for its payment: issue_refund refuses a payment whose dispute_status is won or lost, and the agent never refunds the booking outside the network's decision; a chargeback still open on the same payment is answered once with respond_chargeback (CB-OPEN), and answering it pays nothing.
- [MUST CB-WINDOW] [V1] respond_chargeback answers a chargeback only before its respond_by, set from the network's response_days (30 calendar days for Visa [V1], 45 for Mastercard [V2]); after it the dispute stays with the cardholder and the agent records the missed window with add_note.
- [MUST CB-VENDOR-FAULT] [synthetic] Where the vendor failed to deliver the booking (a cancel_event by airline, vendor or property whatever its cause, weather included, or cause supplier_fault), the agent accepts the chargeback with respond_chargeback decision accept and never contests it; it contests only where the customer owes the amount under the fare_rules or property_policy (the service was delivered, or the customer cancelled late or did not show).
- [MUST CB-EVIDENCE] [synthetic] A respond_chargeback contest carries evidence read from the booking: the vendor booking status (flown, no_show, completed), the fare class and its fare_rules or the property_policy, and the cancel_event.
- [MUST CB-RECOVER] [synthetic] A chargeback accepted for a vendor's fault (CB-VENDOR-FAULT), by the agent with respond_chargeback or before, is a supplier loss: the agent records it with reclassify_cancellation cause supplier_fault and claims exactly the chargeback amount from that vendor in one vendor_send with request_type chargeback_recovery (IP-3); an open chargeback is not yet a loss, and other requests to the vendor (compensation, care, bag or information) are not recovery claims and follow their own rules.
- [SHOULD CB-FCBA] [V1] A customer who asks how to dispute a charge should be told that the billing error notice must reach the card issuer within 60 days after the first statement that shows the charge, and that Visa lets the issuer file a dispute within 120 calendar days.
- [MUST CARE-DUE] [V1] On a cancelled EU flight, or one whose departure is delayed 2 hours or more (1500 km or less), 3 hours or more (intra-EU over 1500 km, other flights of 1500 to 3500 km) or 4 hours or more (all others), the operating carrier owes meals and refreshments in reasonable relation to the waiting time and two calls or e-mails, and hotel accommodation with transport between airport and hotel when the new departure is at least the day after: compute_care gives these items_due from the departure_delay_min, distance_km, intra_eu, cancel_event and reroute.
- [MUST CARE-EXTRAORDINARY] [V1] Care is owed even when the cause was extraordinary and compute_entitlement gives no compensation; a carrier's refusal of a care_expense_claim is escalated with escalate to supplier_relations with the claimed amount, and TripDesk never pays a care cost itself.
- [MUST CARE-CLAIM] [V1] Where the carrier did not give care, the customer's receipts for it are claimed from the operating carrier through vendor_send with request_type care_expense_claim, for the claimable amount compute_care gives, in the currency it gives; an item the carrier gave in kind (care_provided) or a purchase that is not care is never claimed, and a care claim is never added into a compensation_claim.
- [SHOULD CARE-CHOICE] [V1] On a cancellation or a departure delay of 5 hours or more (reroute_or_refund from compute_care), the agent should tell the customer of the choice between a refund of the ticket within seven days, re-routing at the earliest opportunity and re-routing at a later date.
- [MUST INS-INSURER] [V1] The insurer of a policy (insurer_id in insurance_policies) is never TripDesk: a premium is refunded only by insurance_cancel and a claim is paid only by the insurer through insurance_file_claim, and the agent never pays a premium or a claim with issue_refund or issue_refund_legacy.
- [MUST INS-FREELOOK] [V1] Inside the plan's free look (15 days after purchase, the free_look_days in the insurer's fare_rules, before the trip starts and with no claim filed) insurance_cancel refunds the whole premium; after it the premium is kept, so the agent runs insurance_cancel after the free look only where the message accepts losing the premium (IP-2).
- [MUST INS-REFUNDS-FIRST] [V1] A claim is for the loss no one else pays: before insurance_file_claim the agent takes what law or the vendor owes (compute_entitlement, quote_cancellation, issue_refund) and claims for a booking only its paid amount less the refundable amount quote_cancellation gives, the refunds already paid and any value the vendor returned as a credit (an eCredit, voucher or future flight credit counts as recovered), so a booking cancelled for a credit leaves nothing to claim; insurance_file_claim never pays a credited value.
- [MUST INS-NO-DOUBLE] [synthetic] The agent never files insurance_file_claim for a booking whose payment has an open, won or lost chargeback (dispute_status), since the card network already decides that money.
- [MUST INS-REASON] [V1] The reason of insurance_file_claim is the cause the booking shows: carrier_disruption_weather when the carrier cancelled for weather, carrier_disruption_mechanical when it cancelled for a mechanical failure (covered by Travel Guard only), strike, sickness_or_injury, trip_delay when the carrier delayed the trip without cancelling it, cancel_for_any_reason only on a policy with cfar true, and change_of_mind, which neither plan covers.
- [MUST INS-DELAY] [V1] Trip delay pays only once the delay reaches the plan's trip_delay_min_hours, and at most the plan's trip_delay_daily_max per traveller for each started day of delay: insurance_file_claim caps a trip_delay claim at that.
- [SHOULD INS-UNCOVERED] Before filing a reason the plan's covered_reasons do not list, the agent should tell the customer the insurer will deny it, and should file it only where the message asks for the claim anyway.
- [MUST INS-WINDOW] [V1] insurance_file_claim files a claim with the insurer, never with TripDesk, within 90 days after the loss (the claim_days_after_loss in the insurer's fare_rules, counted from the policy's trip_end).

## 70 Re-routing, rail changes and journey scope

- [MUST GP-CARE-DENIED] [V1] A passenger denied boarding against their will is owed care and the choice of re-routing or refund at once, with or without a recorded delay (EU261 Art 4(3): "the operating air carrier shall immediately compensate them in accordance with Article 7 and assist them in accordance with Articles 8 and 9"): compute_care gives the items_due for a booking whose data has denied_boarding, with hotel and transport when the reroute departs on a later day.
- [MUST GP-JOURNEY] [V2] The flights of one through ticket (ticket_group) are one journey (CJEU C-537/17 Wegener: "those flights constitute a whole for the purposes of the right to compensation"): compute_entitlement and compute_care judge every leg by the region of the journey's first flight, never by the leg's own region.
- [MUST GP-REROUTE] [V1] When the airline cancelled the flight (a cancel_event by airline), denied boarding or caused a missed connection (missed_connection), a passenger who does not take the refund is re-routed at no cost with flight_reroute: on the airline's own flight, or on another airline (new_vendor_id) only where EU261 or UK261 applies (Art 8(1)(b): "re-routing, under comparable transport conditions, to their final destination at the earliest opportunity") or the airline's fare_rules give reroute_other_carrier, every condition it lists (when) holds and new_vendor_id is one of its partners, and only when the airline has no flight of its own on the route arriving as early. Delta's contract leaves another carrier to "Delta's sole discretion" (Rule 19.A), so it gives no such right.
- [SHOULD GP-REROUTE-EARLIEST] The agent should re-route on the earliest arriving flight flight_search returns, narrowed with arrive_by to the time the customer must arrive, and record a refused request for another airline with add_note.
- [MUST GP-RAIL-CHANGE] [V1] rail_change moves a confirmed Deutsche Bahn reservation before departure to a later train on the same route by the fare rule in the vendor's fare_rules: a Flexpreis changes free until the day before its first day of validity and for 30 EUR from that day, a Sparpreis for 10 EUR [V2] only before its first day of validity, and a Super Sparpreis never; the change fee plus the fare_difference is charged to the original payment only within the max_fare_difference the message authorizes (IP-2).
- [MUST GP-AMTRAK-CHANGE] [V1] rail_change moves a confirmed Amtrak Flex reservation before departure to a later train on the same route with "No fee, difference in fare may apply", the fare_difference charged only within the max_fare_difference the message authorizes (IP-2), and never changes an Amtrak Value fare ("Changes are not permitted").
- [MUST GP-RAIL-REROUTE] [V1] When the operator cancelled the train (a cancel_event by vendor) or its own late train made the passenger miss the reserved one (missed_connection), the operator rebooks the passenger on its next available train (Amtrak: "Every reasonable effort will be made to rebook you on the next available service"; EU rail: Regulation 2021/782 Art 18 [V2]) at no cost [synthetic]: rail_change makes that change free, also after the booked departure, and the agent takes the earliest train rail_search returns.
- [MUST GP-RAIL-EXCHANGE] [synthetic] A passenger who accepted the operator's move to another train after a cancellation (exchanged_from on the reservation, exchange_accepted true) holds the new ticket: its own fare rules decide a later cancellation (CV-DB-FARE, CV-CANCEL-MONEY), and the cancelled train gives no refund right on it.
- [MUST GP-LH-LIGHT] [V2] A Lufthansa Light fare is kept in full on a voluntary cancellation and its unused taxes are refunded (General Conditions of Carriage Art 10.3.1 [V1]: "an amount equal to the fare paid, less any reasonable service charges or cancellation fees"): flight_cancel and quote_cancellation keep the booking's total less its taxes and return the taxes as cash.
- [MUST GP-FR-REGULAR] [V2] A Ryanair Regular fare is non-refundable (terms 10.1: "all amounts you pay for flights operated by us (including amounts for optional services) are non-refundable"), and a passenger who does not travel gets the government taxes back less the tax refund administration fee (terms 4.2), 20 EUR [V2]: flight_cancel and quote_cancellation keep the total less the taxes plus the tax_refund_fee in the vendor's fare_rules, and return the rest as cash.
- [MUST GP-NO-TERMS] [synthetic] A write tool changes nothing and answers with an error when the booking lacks the terms or times it applies (hotel_cancel without a property_policy, flight_cancel without cancel terms for the fare, cruise_cancel without a cancel_schedule band, rail_change for a fare with no change_fee): the agent never guesses the terms, and asks the vendor with vendor_send only where it still needs them (a refund the law gives, such as P-DOT-REFUND, needs no fare terms).
- [MUST GP-VENDOR-REPLY] [synthetic] A vendor answers vendor_send from its own records of the booking: KU's refund_status reply names the refund it paid on that booking or says none was paid, and River North answers a fee_waiver by its terms (SC-RIVERNORTH) and refuses it, so the agent tells the customer the fee stands.
